What we'll diagnose

Five parts of how your expertise becomes something a buyer can choose.

  1. Service

    Is what you're selling actually clear? We'll examine what you're offering, who it's for, the problem it solves, what the client receives, and what they're ultimately paying for.

  2. Buyer

    Does your service connect to a problem your buyer actually cares about? We'll look at the situation they're in, what they're trying to achieve, what they've tried already, and whether your offer makes sense from their perspective.

  3. Message & signals

    What does your current presence tell people about you? We'll examine relevant parts of your profile, content, offer, proof and calls to action to understand what a potential buyer is likely to take away.

  4. Trust

    Is there enough evidence to make someone comfortable choosing you? We'll identify what is currently building trust, and where additional clarity or evidence may be needed.

  5. Conversion

    What happens when someone becomes interested? We'll look at the path from interest to inquiry to working with you, and identify unnecessary friction or confusion.

Why this approach?

More activity isn't always the answer.

If your service is unclear, more content won't necessarily fix it. If the wrong people are seeing your message, more visibility won't necessarily fix it. If people understand what you do but don't trust the offer, posting more won't necessarily fix it. And if qualified people are interested but don't know what to do next, more followers won't necessarily fix it.

First, we find where the breakdown is. Then we decide what deserves your attention.

This is not a 90-minute business makeover.

We're not going to redesign your entire business, create months of content, or solve every problem at once. We're going to identify the most important constraint and determine what you should focus on now. The goal is clarity you can actually act on.
