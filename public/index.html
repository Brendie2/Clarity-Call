We diagnose before we prescribe.

The Market Perception Diagnostic is a structured 90-minute working session designed to help you identify the 1 main constraint between your expertise and a clear, hireable service.

We will not try to fix your entire business in 90 minutes. Instead, we will focus on the most useful question: What is actually preventing your expertise from becoming a service buyers understand, value, and hire?

Maybe your service is hard to understand. Maybe you have not established who needs it. Maybe the offer is clear, but buyers are not seeing enough evidence, or you are not reaching them at all. We will look at your situation, your evidence, and your current market-facing materials to identify the most important constraint, not simply the one that feels most frustrating.

What we will diagnose

Different situations. Different next steps.

Your challenge may fall into one or more of these areas. We will use the session to identify where the strongest issue appears to be, not assume that messaging fixes everything.

1. Skills-to-offer uncertainty

You have skills and experience, but you do not know what service to sell.

2. Buyer or demand uncertainty

You have an idea, but you do not know who needs it or whether they will pay for it.

3. Offer clarity problem

People cannot quickly understand what you do, who it is for, or what they receive.

4. Trust and perception problem

People may understand your service, but they do not yet see why they should choose you.

5. Buyer-access or sales problem

Your offer may be reasonably clear, but you are not reaching enough of the right buyers or converting interest into conversations.

6. Delivery or capability gap

You may need to strengthen your skills, proof, or delivery confidence before selling a particular service.
